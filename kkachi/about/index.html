About

We only report what holds.

Project KKACHI builds agents for security teams who are tired of proving which alerts are real. Our rule is simple: if we cannot reproduce it, we do not report it.

Ask for Early access ↗ or write to us ↗

Principles

Proof over volume

One reproduced finding is worth more than a thousand warnings.

Keep what was ruled out

Rejected paths are recorded with their reasons, so the next look starts further ahead.

People decide

Agents find and prove. People decide what is disclosed, fixed, or retested.

Approved scope only

We test only what its owner has approved, and stop at the edge of that scope.

The name

Kkachi (까치) is Korean for magpie, the bird said to bring good news.

Our visual language comes from najeon, the Korean craft of setting mother-of-pearl into black lacquer: light set firmly into the dark, one piece at a time.
