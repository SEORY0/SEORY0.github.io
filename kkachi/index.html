The Signal

KKACHI separates guessing from knowing.

Every suspicion becomes a hypothesis. The agent traces it from entry point to sink, then replays the crash three times in a fresh sandbox. If it fails once, it is dropped, and the reason is kept.

  • No source is not a blocker.
  • Each finding shows the call path from input to crash.
  • Three clean replays in a new VM, or it is not a finding.
  • Nothing is disclosed until a person approves it.
